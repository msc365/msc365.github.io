**MSC** stands for **M**artin **S**winkels in the **C**loud. It is the name I use for my work.

The **365** in msc365.eu is inspired by Microsoft 365. When I picked the domain, most of my days were spent in SharePoint, Teams, Entra ID and the Power Platform, building business apps, workflows and integrations. Over the years I moved into Azure and DevOps. I started with Azure DevOps, and today I work mainly in GitHub.

The work is wider than Microsoft 365 now, so these days I just say MSC. I kept msc365.eu as the home of this site. My side project has its own domain, msckite.com, with the same prefix.
